M.A. Student in Communication, Class of 2026, School of Journalism and Communication, Nanjing University.

## About

My research interests lie in media philosophy and the intellectual history of communication. I am also interested in media practices and sociocultural questions shaped by technological change. My experience in reporting, convergent-media production, and social-media operations informs an ongoing effort to connect theoretical inquiry with contemporary media practice.

## Research Interests

- Media Philosophy
- Intellectual History of Communication
- Media Technology and Sociocultural Change
- Journalism and Communication Practice
